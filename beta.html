This is a prototype, not a release. It is a design study for a phone app — laid out at 390 points wide and never tested beyond that. Nothing here is wired to a backend, the figures are the same static tables the decks read from, and layouts above tablet width are undefined. The three production decks are unaffected by anything on this page.

Four tabs

What's in the build
01

Journey

A continuous scroll from the Sun out to Neptune at 30 AU. Each body opens a plate with the same figures the Solar System deck carries.

02

Moons

Twenty-two satellites across five systems. Tapping one raises a sheet rather than navigating away, so you keep your place in the system.

03

Gallery

Thirty JunoCam frames developed by volunteers from raw pushframe strips. Frames open into a loupe for close reading.

04

Colophon

Provenance for every figure and image — NASA Planetary Fact Sheets, IAU WGCCRE 2015, and the Mission Juno processing gallery.

Known gaps

Before you file it
  • Desktop and tablet widths are unstyled — the shell stretches instead of centring, which is why it is framed at phone size here.
  • No deep links. Refreshing always returns you to the top of the Journey.
  • The gallery loupe has no pinch-zoom yet; it scales to fit only.
  • Everything is bundled into one 12 MB file, so the first load is heavy and then entirely offline.
