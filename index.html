Start at the Sun and go out to Neptune. Each world gets one photograph filling the screen and the few numbers that define it: its size, its mass, its gravity, the length of its day, its tilt, its temperature and its air.

Jupiter, developed by volunteers

Last, one planet up close. JunoCam sends home raw strips, and members of the public turn them into pictures. This deck shows how the camera works, and why no two pictures of Jupiter agree.
